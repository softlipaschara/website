    <b>3 metaphors from the physical world:<br/></b>
    1\. When you set a fire, you could blow towards it to make the air flow, the fire will be brighter and stronger.<br/>
    2\. A sudden and strong blow makes the candle out.<br/>
    3\. The flame can be passed from one to another spot.
