    <b>Teamwork:</b><br /> Xinyue Yang, Antonia Nandori<br /><br />
    <b>Problems:</b><br /> Coffee consumption on the go increasingly relies on self-service.
    Starbucks-style chain outlets make us stand in line to order; our workplace cafeteria
    requires us to operate an automated machine. While this optimizes the process for the
    organisation who sell the coffee, for the consumer, it becomes ever more tiresome. We spend
    longer queueing, are under pressure to speed up our order, and know very little about the
    product they will receive. This amounts to a dissatisfactory user experience, one which
    falls far short of traditional café service. <br /><br /><br />

    <p style="text-align:center;"> The interaction is boring - pressing a button, and user knows little about what they will receive </p><br /><br /><br />
    <b>Idea:</b><br /> Coffee Composer puts the fun back into self-service. This service design
    concept enriches the interaction for consumers, making the most of what humans do best: the
    creative and tactile parts of the process. A range of color-coded ‘tokens’ symbolize
    possible ingredients (such as milk and sugar), <b>offering users greater transparency and
    autonomy</b> to choose what ends up in their coffee. A glass cup makes it easy for users to
    comprehend the custom combination of ingredients as they are dispensed.<br /><br />
    <b>The selection process begins earlier, meaning queue time is used more effectively,</b> as
    customers get to pick and stack their preferred elements to create their ideal hot beverage.
    The automated coffee machine intuitively ‘reads’ their order, converting each token into its
    corresponding ingredient in consecutive order.
    <b>The communication between the automated machine and human user becomes more subtle and
    enjoyable.</b><br /><br /> Coffee Composer demonstrates that increased automation need not
    result in a banal and laboured interaction between user and machine - it should instead free
    us humans up to pursue activities we enjoy.<br /><br />
