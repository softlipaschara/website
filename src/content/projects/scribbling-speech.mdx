    <b>Idea:</b><br />
    Language and images are closely intertwined: We think in pictures and we explain facts as
    spatial constellations. What if the spoken word could be transformed into dynamic visual
    worlds in real time? Speech input, machine learning and recurrent neural networks for image
    generation allow to com- puter generate complex imaginary worlds that follow the narrator
    and thus create complex animations controlled by linguistic structures.<br /><br />

<div class="containerCenter" style="margin-top:10%;">
  <h2>Input - Spoken Words</h2>
</div>
<div class="containerCenter">
  <div class="projectContent projectTextInsBorrow">
    <br /><br /><br /><b>Real time Voice Recognition and Multi language Detection</b><br /><br />
    With Google Speech API and Google Natural Language API, it is able to extract nouns and
    adpostions like "above","under" and so on from the real time streaming. RecognizeStream,
    getEntities and analyzeSyntax are used to realize the input part.<br /><br />
    For example. "There is a bird flying above the sea", the computer will return "sea", "bird"
    and "above" in the end.<br /><br />
    Besides, not only English can be recognized, but also many other languages like Chinese,
    French, German and so on.
  </div>
</div>

<div class="containerCenter" style="margin-top:10%;">
  <h2>Output - Animated Drawings</h2>
</div>
<div class="containerCenter">
  <div class="projectContent projectTextInsBorrow">
    <br /><br /><b>Quickdraw Dataset</b><br /><br />
    Quick, Draw! is an AI experiment from Google that has delighted millions of people across
    the world. The game asks users to draw a doodle, then the game’s AI tries to guess what it
    is.<br /><br />
    The richness and the huge amount of data as well as the simplicity of human doodlings, which
    contains a common understanding of human depicted common objects. It is a visual language of
    stereotypes of flower”, “mountain”, “cloud”, “river” etc. Over a billion doodles have been
    drawn by people playing the game and subsequently collected into this anonymized dataset.
  </div>
</div>
