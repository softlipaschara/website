    <b>Tools: <br /></b> Google Speech API, Google Natural Language API, Unity, Asset store
    <br />
    <br />
    <b>Idea:</b><br /> “Word to World” is a further development of my bachelor thesis
    “Scribbling Speech”. It is a software that transforms real-time speech into dynamic
    visualisation. I used Unity game engine to process the data passing from the language
    input, the computer outputs a corresponding three-dimensional visual world where camera
    movements, physics, forces, collisions, animations, and motions are working together. We
    are proficient at intuition, language, imagination, and creativity, while the computer is
    proficient at computation, algorithm, logic, and memory, "Word to World" created a mix of
    intuition and logic. It encourages creation with technology.<br /><br /><br /><br />
  </div>
</div>
